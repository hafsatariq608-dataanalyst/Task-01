Predicting Organic Traffic Decay and Automated Content Refresh Opportunities

Author: Hafsa Tariq | Lane: Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring | Date: August 2026

Abstract

How can digital publishers systematically prioritize outdated or decaying content for editorial refresh before significant organic traffic loss occurs? We evaluated a heuristic rule-based opportunity engine against a Random Forest classification model trained on anonymized historical Search Console and traffic performance features. The Random Forest model achieved a superior ROC-AUC compared to the static rule baseline on an 80/20 stratified holdout split. Model predictions were mapped into automated action labels (REWRITE_AND_UPDATE, MONITOR) with interpretable reason codes. This framework provides a decision-support mechanism to replace manual content auditing with automated, score-ranked editorial queues.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

Editorial capacity is inherently finite, while digital publication archives continuously grow. Manual auditing across thousands of URLs is inefficient and prone to subjective bias. This work supports editorial resource allocation by scoring and ranking content pieces based on observed statistical patterns of performance decay.

2. Data Specification

Built on the content_refresh_anonymized.csv release. Granularity is 1 row per unique content piece snapshot. trend_pct was deliberately excluded to prevent target leakage. Zero-position values were explicitly cleaned to NaN.

3. Methodology & Validation

The target proxy identifies high-priority decaying content (trend_direction == 'down' AND ctr < median_ctr). Evaluated using an 80/20 Stratified Train/Test split across decision-moment knowable features (impressions_90d, clicks_90d, ctr, Clean Position, Content Age, Word Count).

5. Limitations & Framing

This work measures observed statistical correlations and directional performance indicators. It does not claim causal proof of search engine ranking algorithms or guarantee post-refresh traffic recovery.

6. Ranked Recommendations

Articles scoring ≥ 60.0 are assigned to REWRITE_AND_UPDATE with reason codes (e.g., TRAFFIC_DECLINE|LOW_CTR). Items scoring < 60.0 are assigned to MONITOR.
